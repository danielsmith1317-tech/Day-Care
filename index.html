Property selection criteria

Do not commit based on square footage or a commercial listing alone. Verify zoning, playground area, circulation, safety and practical licensing capacity for each site.

Search strategy

Start with existing buildings in the Parrish/Moccasin Wallow area and consider lease, renovation, purchase or ground-up construction only after preliminary feasibility. The earlier 2,500 ft² gross concept is not approved for 60 children. An architect and licensing office must assess how much space is usable.

Do not treat earlier property advertisements, prices, or apparent availability as current or as evidence that childcare use is permitted.
